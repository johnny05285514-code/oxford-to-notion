# Oxford to Notion v1.5.4

## 简体中文

- 调整“关于与更新”“使用偏好”“帮助”等分区标题的字重。
- 为分区标题增加少量顶部留白，改善 Windows 中文粗体在小字号下的笔画渲染。
- 这是首个可从 v1.5.3 软件内发现、下载并打开安装程序的更新版本。
- 个人 Notion 配置和最近导入记录会继续保留。

## English

- Refined the weight of section headings such as About and updates, Preferences, and Help.
- Added a small amount of top breathing room to improve Chinese text rendering on Windows.
- This is the first release that v1.5.3 can discover and download through the in-app update flow.
- Existing Notion configuration and recent import history remain preserved.
