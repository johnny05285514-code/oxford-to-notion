# Oxford to Notion v1.6.0

## 简体中文

- 批量导入：每次最多 20 个不同的词，支持换行、逗号和分号分隔，逐个处理并单独重试失败项。
- 单词导入显示实际阶段及重试次数，失败后可重试原单词。
- 历史菜单同时提供 Oxford 和 Notion 打开入口。
- 保存成功但旧内容清理失败时提供“仅重试清理”；正文未变化时跳过替换。
- 关闭窗口时等待当前导入或同步结束；清理较旧安装包缓存，保留最近一份旧包。
- 支持 Windows 和 Apple 芯片 Mac。个人笔记请放在软件管理的 Oxford 区域之外。

## English

- Import up to 20 unique words sequentially, separated by newlines, commas or semicolons, with per-word results and retries.
- See actual import stages and retry counts, and retry failed words.
- Open either Oxford or Notion from history context menus.
- Retry incomplete cleanup without rewriting saved content; skip unchanged dictionary body replacements.
- Wait for active imports and sync on close, and prune old update packages while retaining one older package.
- Available for Windows and Apple silicon macOS. Keep personal notes outside the app-managed Oxford section.
